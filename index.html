Waypoint 01 · Where you are

Stuck isn't a character flaw. It's a starting point.

The Lit Path is the coaching practice of Robbert Ferd. It exists for one reason: most people don't need to be fixed — they need a clear-eyed partner while they figure out what they actually want, and a structure that keeps them moving toward it.

Career crossroads, a relationship reset, a habit you can't seem to break alone, a goal that keeps sliding to next year — coaching meets you exactly where you are. No judgment about how you got there. The work is entirely about the road ahead.

Sessions are one-on-one, confidential, and practical. You leave each one with something concrete: a decision made, a next step named, a commitment you actually intend to keep.

Waypoint 02 · The work

Three steps onto the path.

Step 01

Discovery call

A free, no-pressure conversation. You talk about where you are and where you'd like to be; we decide together whether coaching is the right tool — and whether we're the right fit.

Step 02

Map the route

We turn "something has to change" into a destination you can describe out loud — specific goals, honest obstacles, and the values that make the goals worth reaching.

Step 03

Walk it together

Regular one-on-one sessions with real accountability in between. Progress gets reviewed, the plan gets adjusted, and momentum stops depending on motivation alone.

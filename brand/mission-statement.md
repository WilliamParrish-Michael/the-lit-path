# The Lit Path — Mission statement (client-supplied, 2026-10-19)

Transcribed verbatim from the client's "The Lit Path.docx". Spelling/grammar as supplied.

To provide services that connect people with treatment facilities and guarantee a place in there center. Extensive work with the families of the addict and what to expect on the return home and hope to help them cope with a newly sober addict and what to look for such as key things like isolation and suicidal behavior in their loved ones. Sober companionship along with the newly sober individual such as flying across country to make sure that the recovering addict makes it to their destination with staff that are certified to handle things such as over doses and relapse behavior and making sure the recovering addict is socializing.

- Help the Family of the addict reconnect with the sick member of their family in a group setting
- Identify all the behavior of the addict from people that have been there and made it to the other side
- Convince with the support of the family that the life they have been living is a way to die and not a way to live
- Help the family reassert boundaries with the sick individual not just for mental health but physical safety
- A sober companion with experience that knows what it is like to live that way to be with them from start to finish on there journey from decision to destination.
- To make sure the family is heavily supported with questions and concern while there loved one is being treated for their illness
- Do everything it takes to convince the sick that being sober is the right choice in life from people that reflect what they would one day hope to be

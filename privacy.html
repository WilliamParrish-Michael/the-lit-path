Privacy policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email address, optional phone number, and your message. That's it — this site has no accounts, no tracking pixels, and no analytics scripts of its own.

How the form works

Form submissions are relayed by FormSubmit, a third-party service, and delivered to us by email. FormSubmit processes your submission for the sole purpose of delivering it; their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policy.

How we use your information

We use your contact details only to respond to your inquiry and, if you choose to work with us, to schedule and conduct coaching sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) as part of serving it.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces; loading them transmits standard request data to Google.
  • FormSubmit relays contact form submissions as described above.

Confidentiality of coaching

Anything discussed in a coaching relationship is treated as confidential and is not published, shared, or used in marketing without your explicit written permission — including testimonials.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold from your correspondence or to request that it be deleted. Email Robbertferd@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, phone number, optional email address, optionally who you are calling about, and your message. If you share a story through the "share your story" form, we receive the name and text you enter. That's it — this site has no accounts, no tracking pixels, and no analytics scripts of its own.

How the form works

Form submissions are relayed by FormSubmit, a third-party service, and delivered to us by email. FormSubmit processes your submission for the sole purpose of delivering it; their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policy.

How we use your information

We use your contact details only to respond to your inquiry and, if you choose to work with us, to arrange the services you ask about. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) as part of serving it.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces; loading them transmits standard request data to Google.
  • FormSubmit relays contact form submissions as described above.
  • Render and Neon host the small service that stores submitted stories and blog posts, when it is in use.

Sensitive information and confidentiality

Families often tell us about a loved one's substance use, mental health, or safety. Share only what you are comfortable sharing; you do not need to include health details to reach us. Anything you tell us is treated as confidential and is not published, shared, or used in marketing without your explicit written permission — including stories and testimonials. Please do not submit information about another adult that they would not want shared unless you are seeking help for them.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold from your correspondence or to request that it be deleted. Email Robbertferd@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
